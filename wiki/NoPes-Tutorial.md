# NoPes Tutorial

Welcome to **NoPes**! This tutorial will get you up to speed with your new knowledge base.

## Writing Notes

NoPes uses standard Markdown. You can create headings with `#`, lists with `-` or `1.`, and links with `[text](url)`.

## Math & LaTeX

NoPes supports beautiful math rendering using KaTeX. You can write inline math with single dollar signs, like $E = mc^2$, or display math with double dollar signs:

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}$$

The quadratic formula is $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$.

## Linking Your Thinking

Connect notes with WikiLinks: `[[Another Note]]`. Hover over a link to preview it, or click to jump to that note.

## Next Steps

- Explore the **Graph View** (`Cmd+G`) to visualize your knowledge network.
- Use the **Command Bar** (`Cmd+K`) to search notes and run actions.
- Enable **Local AI** in Settings to chat with your vault offline.
